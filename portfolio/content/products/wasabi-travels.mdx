## Independent product

I built Wasabi Travels as an independent product, separate from my employer work. It helps people plan Japan trips without turning every day into a spreadsheet exercise.

## What it does

The planner uses more than 2,000 curated places to suggest routes and check the daily practicality of an itinerary. I focus on helping travellers move from saved places to a sequence that makes sense on the ground.

## Product principle

I want planning support to make a trip more considered, not more complicated. Suggestions are a starting point for the traveller’s choices.
