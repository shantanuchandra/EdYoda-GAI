## Independent product

I am building Card Compass as an independent product, separate from my employer work. It is designed to make card comparisons easier to reason about.

## What it does

Card Compass considers spending patterns and reward rules when presenting recommendations. It received 121 early-access requests.

## Product principle

I aim to make the factors behind a recommendation understandable, so people can decide whether it fits their own spending rather than accept a black-box answer.
