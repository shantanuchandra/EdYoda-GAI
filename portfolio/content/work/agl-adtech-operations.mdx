## Context

As Senior Product Manager for AdTech, I mentored two PMs and worked across Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, and two national FMCG clients.

## Opportunity

I focused on replacing repetitive campaign operations with reliable automation while making onboarding and performance reporting easier to use at scale.

## My role

I shaped the product work, mentored PMs, and partnered with teams responsible for campaign operations, platform execution, and client adoption.

## Approach

We automated budgets, bids, campaign rules, inventory checks, and reporting across the operating workflow.

## Governance

I treated automation as an operating system with defined rules and checks, so teams could inspect campaign conditions and reporting rather than rely on opaque changes.

## Adoption

Eligible-user adoption reached 50% in one quarter, while onboarding moved from several months to one week.

## Outcomes

Manual campaign work decreased 70%. In the rollout, managed-campaign ROAS moved from 1.3× to 1.7× over eight months, a 31% increase; I present this as a rollout result rather than sole attribution.

## Lessons

I learned that operational automation needs clear rules, observable checks, and a practical onboarding path before teams will trust it in daily campaign work.
