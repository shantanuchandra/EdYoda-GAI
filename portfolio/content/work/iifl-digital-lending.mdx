## Context

As AVP / Lead Product Manager, I managed six PMs and led 25 engineers and data scientists across three squads. The app handled about one million onboardings each month.

## Opportunity

I saw an opportunity to shorten employed-customer onboarding and approval while making policy knowledge easier for field-sales teams to access responsibly.

## My role

I led the product direction across the squads, connecting customer experience, operating controls, adoption, and measurable delivery outcomes.

## Approach

We reduced employed-customer onboarding and approval from one week to 20 minutes. I also led a RAG assistant pilot for 2,000 field-sales employees to support policy questions in their daily work.

## Governance

About 80% of roughly 100 weekly questions cleared a 95% confidence threshold; remaining questions routed to policy specialists. Customer communications and collections required human approval and an audit trail, while KYC and AML practices aligned with RBI and NHB requirements.

## Adoption

The field-sales pilot put a clear specialist route beside the assistant so teams could use it for suitable questions without treating it as the final authority.

## Outcomes

The onboarding flow moved from one week to 20 minutes, and the assistant reached a 2,000-employee pilot with a defined confidence threshold and escalation path.

## Lessons

I learned that speed in a regulated workflow has to be paired with explicit approvals, traceability, and a dependable route for questions the system should not answer alone.
