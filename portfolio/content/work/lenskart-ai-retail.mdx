## Context

As AI Product Lead reporting to a co-founder, I worked with a team of three AI engineers, two ML engineers, and one DevOps engineer to select and bring AI-supported retail journeys into stores.

## Opportunity

I focused on making useful assistance practical at the point of need: voice-guided eye tests, faster intent detection, and recruiting checks that could remove repetitive work without removing professional judgment.

## My role

I set product direction, release criteria, and the operating decisions needed to move each use case from evaluation into a responsible rollout.

## Approach

We launched a Hindi and English voice-guided eye test in 200 stores. I also helped introduce on-device intent detection that was three times faster, and recruiter sourcing and interest checks that reduced a process for 300 candidates from about eight weeks to one week.

## Governance

An optometrist approved every final prescription, and low-confidence cases received human review. I defined release criteria around accuracy, human agreement, reliability, response time, and cost.

## Adoption

I worked with the store and recruiting workflows so the AI support fit existing decisions instead of asking teams to work around it. Human review stayed visible in the experience.

## Outcomes

Across 1,000 controlled and 1,000 live-store tests, results reached 95% agreement within accepted industry tolerance. The store rollout covered 200 locations while keeping final prescriptions optometrist-approved.

## Lessons

I learned that retail AI earns trust when the product makes its confidence, escalation path, and professional accountability clear from the beginning.
