## Context

I joined as Product Manager (Design) in December 2020 and moved into Senior Product Manager (Conversational AI) in April 2022, leading four PMs, two designers, and 14 engineers and data scientists across five squads.

## Opportunity

I worked on turning a conversational AI capability into an adopted customer-success workflow that could support much larger onboarding volume.

## My role

I coordinated product direction across the squads and connected customer learning, model-quality standards, sales enablement, and adoption measures.

## Approach

We used customer interviews, betas, demos, and sales enablement to shape the workflow. I also established consistent intent labels, training-data review, and model evaluation standards.

## Governance

Consistent intent labels, training-data review, and model evaluation standards gave teams a shared way to assess quality as the workflow expanded.

## Adoption

Customer-success managers participated through interviews, betas, demos, and enablement. Adoption reached 90% among 150 customer-success managers.

## Outcomes

Completed monthly onboardings increased from 50 to 25,000 in six months. In month six, 12,500 users bought at least one month of service.

## Lessons

I learned that conversational AI scales when customer learning, sales enablement, and model-quality practices are designed together rather than treated as separate launches.
