# Onboarding, Communications, And Email Templates

## Purpose

Use these templates to run a polished cohort without writing every message from scratch.

Replace bracketed fields before sending.

---

## Communication Cadence

| Timing | Message |
|---|---|
| After enrollment | Welcome and expectations |
| 7 days before Week 1 | Setup checklist |
| 24 hours before each session | Session reminder |
| Within 24 hours after each session | Recording, workbook, assignment |
| Midweek | Submission nudge |
| Before feedback checkpoints | Artifact reminder |
| Week 8 | Final portfolio instructions |
| After cohort | Completion, next steps, testimonial request |

---

## Enrollment Welcome

Subject: Welcome to Production AI PM

```text
Hi [Name],

Welcome to Production AI PM.

Over 8 weeks, you will build a Senior AI PM Portfolio Packet that shows how you would lead a GenAI or agentic product from strategy to launch.

Before Week 1, please:
1. read the learner handbook;
2. set up the core tools;
3. choose one AI product idea from your work or domain;
4. create a private folder for your portfolio artifacts.

This cohort assumes you already understand PM fundamentals. We will focus on AI-specific product judgment, system design, evals, production readiness, and executive communication.

See you in Week 1.
```

## Setup Reminder

Subject: Production AI PM setup checklist

```text
Hi [Name],

Please complete these before our first live session:

- Claude or ChatGPT access
- Research tool access
- Google Docs, Notion, or equivalent
- Google Sheets, Airtable, or equivalent
- n8n, Make, Zapier, or equivalent
- Private folder for your portfolio packet
- One AI product idea to evaluate

If you cannot use a real work problem, use the shared Customer Success Copilot case or one of the fallback capstones in the casebook.

Bring one idea you are willing to critique honestly. The goal in Week 1 is not to defend the idea. It is to test whether it deserves to exist.
```

## Midweek Submission Nudge

Subject: Quick nudge: [Artifact] due [Date]

```text
Hi [Name],

Quick nudge that your [Artifact] is due [Date].

Do not over-polish it. Submit a clear draft with:
- the decision it supports;
- the weakest assumption;
- the main production risk;
- one question you want feedback on.

Rough but specific is better than polished and generic.
```

## Feedback Checkpoint Reminder

Subject: Feedback checkpoint: [Artifact]

```text
Hi [Name],

This week is a feedback checkpoint for [Artifact].

Before submitting, check:
- Is the user and workflow specific?
- Is the AI role justified?
- Are failure modes visible?
- Is the quality bar measurable?
- Is the next decision clear?

Please submit by [deadline].
```

## Falling Behind Message

Subject: Getting you back on track

```text
Hi [Name],

I noticed a couple of artifacts are still pending.

The fastest path back:
1. submit the current artifact first;
2. do not rewrite older work yet;
3. use the portfolio packet template;
4. add only the minimum detail needed for critique.

If you are blocked, reply with the artifact name and the specific decision you are stuck on.
```

## Final Portfolio Instructions

Subject: Final portfolio packet instructions

```text
Hi everyone,

For the final session, please assemble your Senior AI PM Portfolio Packet.

It should include:
1. AI Product Strategy Memo
2. AI PRD
3. Agent/RAG Workflow Prototype
4. Eval Dataset and Rubric
5. Technical Architecture Brief
6. Production Readiness Review
7. Executive Narrative and Interview Story

Label each artifact:
- public;
- private;
- anonymized;
- interview-only.

Prepare a 2-minute executive story. Focus on judgment, tradeoffs, evals, and launch readiness.
```

```text

Your next step is to polish the portfolio packet for the context you care about most:
- internal promotion;
- AI PM interviews;
- public portfolio;
- consulting or advisory credibility;
- stronger AI product leadership in your current role.

Recommended 7-day polish:
Day 1: tighten the strategy memo
Day 2: clean the AI PRD
Day 3: strengthen eval rows
Day 4: sharpen the architecture brief
Day 5: simplify the launch review
Day 6: rehearse the 2-minute story
Day 7: decide what is public, private, or interview-only

Your portfolio should show one thing clearly: you can lead AI products beyond the demo.
```

## Community Guidelines Message

Subject: Cohort norms

```text
Hi everyone,

A few norms for the cohort:

- critique artifacts, not people;
- protect confidential information;
- do not share another learner's work outside the cohort;
- bring specific decisions, not vague questions;
- use AI tools, but do not outsource judgment;
- give feedback that makes the work sharper.

The cohort works best when the room is honest, practical, and specific.
```
