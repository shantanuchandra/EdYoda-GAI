# Facilitator Script - AI Strategy, Moats, and Business Case (Session 2 of 8)

**Subtitle:** Turn an AI idea into a defensible product strategy and business case.  
**Duration:** 120 minutes. **Format:** Live virtual. **Audience:** Senior PMs and product leaders.  
**Spine:** Strategy Lens -> Moat Map -> Business Case Critique -> Strategy Memo.  
**Hands-on share:** ~45% (about 54 minutes).  
**Session artifact:** AI Product Strategy Memo v1.  
**Tools assumed available:** Claude or ChatGPT, Perplexity, shared case, portfolio packet.

---

## Pre-Class Checklist

- [ ] Review 3-5 learner opportunity one-pagers.
- [ ] Prepare shared case strategy memo outline.
- [ ] Prepare examples of model moat, data moat, workflow moat, and distribution moat.
- [ ] Open market research examples.

# The 120-Minute Blended Run Sheet

## Block 0 - Opening (0:00-0:10)

**Say:** "Last week we asked whether an AI idea deserves to exist. Today we ask whether it deserves a roadmap."

Ask chat: "What makes an AI product hard to copy?"

## Block 1 - Strategy Lens: Moats Beyond The Model (0:10-0:35)

Teach four defensibility layers:

1. Workflow depth: the product sits inside repeated work.
2. Data advantage: proprietary context improves outcomes.
3. Distribution: the product reaches users at the moment of need.
4. Learning loop: usage improves the system and roadmap.

Warn against thin wrappers:

**Say:** "If the only hard part is calling a frontier model, you have a feature demo, not a product strategy."

## Block 2 - Applied Walkthrough: Shared Case (0:35-0:55)

Use Customer Success Copilot.

Walk through:

- Business outcome: reduce churn risk and improve expansion timing.
- User workflow: CSM account review and next-best-action planning.
- Data advantage: product usage, support tickets, CRM, call notes.
- Risk: incorrect recommendations, account privacy, hallucinated customer claims.

## Hard Break (0:55-1:05)

Real break. Keep the timer visible.

## Block 3 - Studio Critique: Learner Strategy (1:05-1:42)

Learners revise their capstone idea using the strategy memo structure:

1. Business outcome.
2. User workflow.
3. Advantage.
4. Build / buy / partner.
5. First 90 days.
6. Kill criteria.

Critique 2-3 examples live.

## Block 4 - Artifact Build: Strategy Memo (1:42-1:55)

Learners draft a one-page strategy memo.

Quality bar:

- Names a business outcome.
- Names a workflow.
- Names an advantage.
- Names a risk.
- Names a first release.

## Block 5 - Close (1:55-2:00)

Assignment: submit AI Product Strategy Memo v1 before Session 3.

Next: GenAI System Design for PMs.

---

## Contingencies

**If learner ideas are too broad:** force a narrower workflow.  
**If learners over-index on model choice:** redirect to workflow, data, and distribution.  
**If discussion becomes startup-pitch heavy:** bring it back to senior PM execution: roadmap, risk, and launch constraints.
