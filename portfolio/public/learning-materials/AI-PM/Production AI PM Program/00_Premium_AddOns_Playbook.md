# Premium Add-Ons Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook defines optional premium services that can sit above the core cohort.

Use these to create higher-touch tiers without changing the main curriculum.

---

## Add-On Menu

| Add-on | Best for | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 Capstone Review | Learners who want sharper portfolio artifacts | 45 or 60 minutes |
| Mock AI PM Interview | Learners targeting AI PM roles | 60 minutes |
| Resume, LinkedIn, Portfolio Review | Learners using the cohort for career transition | 60 minutes |
| Advanced Technical Lab Review | Learners building deeper technical prototypes | 60 to 90 minutes |
| Executive Narrative Coaching | Product leaders using the work internally | 45 minutes |

---

## 1:1 Capstone Review

### Inputs

Learner submits:

- strategy memo;
- AI PRD;
- eval plan;
- architecture brief;
- launch readiness review;
- specific question they want answered.

### Session Flow

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0-5 min | Clarify goal |
| 5-20 min | Review strategy and AI necessity |
| 20-35 min | Review system, evals, and launch risk |
| 35-50 min | Portfolio positioning |
| 50-60 min | Next actions |

### Output

Short written note:

```text
Strongest portfolio signal:
Weakest assumption:
Most important production risk:
Artifact to improve first:
Next three actions:
```

## Mock AI PM Interview

### Interview Areas

- AI product strategy;
- system design for PMs;
- RAG and agent tradeoffs;
- evals and quality;
- cost and latency;
- safety and launch readiness;
- cross-functional leadership.

### Question Bank

1. Walk me through your AI product capstone.
2. Why does this need AI?
3. What would the non-AI version be?
4. What data does the system need?
5. Where would you use RAG?
6. Where would you allow autonomy?
7. What evals would block launch?
8. What is the biggest production risk?
9. How would you work with engineering?
10. What would you ship first?

### Feedback Output

```text
Interview readiness:
Best answer:
Weakest answer:
Technical clarity:
Product judgment:
Recommended practice:
```

## Tier Packaging Ideas

Use neutral tier names.

| Tier | Includes |
|---|---|
| Core Cohort | Live sessions, workbooks, decks, portfolio packet |
| Portfolio Cohort | Core + 1:1 capstone review |
| Career Cohort | Core + mock interview + resume/LinkedIn/portfolio review |
| Technical Cohort | Core + advanced lab review |
| Executive Cohort | Core + capstone review + executive narrative coaching |

Avoid overloading the core cohort. Premium add-ons should create leverage, not operational chaos.

---

## Operational Rules

- Schedule add-ons only after the relevant artifact exists.
- Require pre-work at least 24 hours before the session.
- Keep each add-on focused on one outcome.
- Send a short written summary after the session.
- Do not rewrite learner artifacts for them.
- Keep confidentiality rules active.

---

## Add-On Booking Questions

1. What artifact do you want reviewed?
2. What decision are you stuck on?
3. What is your target outcome?
4. Is this for internal use, interview use, or public portfolio use?
5. What must remain private?

---

## Success Bar

A premium add-on is successful when the learner leaves with:

- one sharper artifact;
- one clearer story;
- one visible technical or strategic tradeoff;
- one next action they can execute within 48 hours.
