# Production AI PM - Session 1 Workbook

## AI Product Judgment for Senior PMs

Today you turn AI enthusiasm into product judgment.

By the end of this 120-minute session, you will have an **AI Opportunity One-Pager** for a product idea you may use as your capstone.

One rule for today: **Do not defend the AI idea. Test whether it deserves to exist.**
