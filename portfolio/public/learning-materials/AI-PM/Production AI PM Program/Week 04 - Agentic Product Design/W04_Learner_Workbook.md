## Tool Design Checklist

For every tool/action:

- What can the AI read?
- What can the AI write?
- What can the AI trigger?
- What is out of scope?
- What argument validation is needed?
- What should be logged?

---

## Prototype Quality Bar

Your prototype or sketch must show:

- [ ] at least 4 workflow steps;
- [ ] at least 1 model decision;
- [ ] at least 1 tool/data dependency;
- [ ] at least 1 human approval gate;
- [ ] at least 1 failure recovery path;
- [ ] at least 1 telemetry event.
