# Production AI PM - Session 7 Workbook

## Production Readiness: Cost, Latency, Safety, and Launch

Today you decide what must be true before your AI product should ship.

By the end of this 120-minute session, you will have a **Cost and Latency Model**, **Risk Register**, and **Launch Readiness Review**.

One rule for today: **A product is not ready because the demo works. It is ready when the launch gates are clear.**
