# Facilitator Script - AI Product Judgment for Senior PMs (Session 1 of 8)

**Subtitle:** Decide which AI ideas deserve to be built and which should be killed early.  
**Duration:** 120 minutes. **Format:** Live virtual. **Audience:** Senior PMs and product leaders building AI product judgment.  
**Spine:** Judgment Reset -> Opportunity Filter -> Shared Case Critique -> Capstone Selection.  
**Hands-on share:** ~45% (about 54 minutes of learner activity).  
**Session artifact:** AI Opportunity One-Pager.  
**Tools assumed available:** Claude or ChatGPT, Perplexity, shared workbook, portfolio packet template.

---

## Pre-Class Checklist

- [ ] Send learner workbook and portfolio packet template.
- [ ] Ask learners to bring one AI product idea from work or a domain they understand.
- [ ] Prepare 3 example ideas: one strong AI product, one workflow automation, one weak AI wrapper.
- [ ] Pre-load shared B2B SaaS Customer Success Copilot case.
- [ ] Open parking lot doc and visible timer.

# The 120-Minute Blended Run Sheet

## Block 0 - Opening And Promise (0:00-0:10)

**Say:** "Most AI product failures do not start with the model. They start with a PM choosing the wrong problem."

Ask chat: "In one phrase, where do AI ideas usually go wrong in your company?"

Read 5-6 answers. Bridge to the core promise:

**Say:** "Today is not about making every idea sound AI-ready. It is about building the judgment to reject weak ideas before they waste engineering time."

## Block 1 - Strategy Lens: The AI Opportunity Filter (0:10-0:35)

Teach the six-part filter:

1. Pain: frequent, expensive, or high-stakes.
2. Workflow: AI changes the work, not just the UI.
3. Context: the system can access the information needed.
4. Quality tolerance: the user can handle probabilistic output.
5. Economics: value exceeds inference, review, and operational cost.
6. Risk: failure modes are understandable and controllable.

**Studio prompt:** "Score your own idea from 1-5 on each dimension. Do not average the score yet. Circle the weakest dimension."

## Block 2 - Applied Walkthrough: Three Ideas (0:35-0:55)

Show 3 examples:

- Customer Success Copilot for B2B SaaS.
- "AI dashboard summarizer" with no workflow integration.
- Automated refund approval agent.

For each, ask:

- Is AI needed?
- Is workflow automation enough?
- What would make this unsafe?
- What evidence would change our mind?

## Hard Break (0:55-1:05)

Real break. Keep the timer visible.

## Block 3 - Studio Critique: Learner Ideas (1:05-1:40)

Put learners in pairs or small groups.

Each learner presents:

1. User and workflow.
2. Current pain.
3. Why AI might help.
4. Weakest part of the opportunity filter.
5. One non-AI alternative.

Critique rule: peers must improve the idea or kill it. No polite vagueness.

## Block 4 - Artifact Build: Opportunity One-Pager (1:40-1:55)

Learners draft:

- Problem.
- Target user.
- Workflow.
- Why AI.
- Why not simple automation.
- Key data/context.
- Main risk.
- Decision: pursue, reshape, or kill.

## Block 5 - Close (1:55-2:00)

**Say:** "A senior AI PM is not the person with the most AI ideas. It is the person who knows which ideas deserve engineering time."

Assignment: complete the AI Opportunity One-Pager before Session 2.

Next: AI Strategy, Moats, and Business Case.

---

## Contingencies

**If learners bring weak ideas:** use that as the lesson. Make the rewrite the artifact.  
**If no one shares:** critique the 3 prepared examples in more depth.  
**If tool access fails:** run the session entirely from workbook and shared doc.
