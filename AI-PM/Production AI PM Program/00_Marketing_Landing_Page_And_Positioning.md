## Objection Handling

### "I do not code. Can I still join?"

Yes. The core path is no-code / low-code. You will learn enough technical fluency to work credibly with AI/ML teams, define evals, and reason about system tradeoffs. Optional advanced labs are available for learners who want API-level depth.

### "Is this just prompt engineering?"

No. Prompts are treated as one part of product behavior. The program focuses on product strategy, system design, RAG and agent workflows, evals, cost, latency, safety, launch readiness, and executive communication.

### "I already know PM fundamentals."

Good. The program assumes that. We spend little time on generic PM basics and focus on the AI-specific judgment senior PMs need.

### "Can I use a work problem?"

Yes, if you can do so safely. The program includes confidentiality rules and encourages anonymized artifacts when needed.

### "Will I have something to show at the end?"

Yes. The final output is a Senior AI PM Portfolio Packet with seven artifacts.

## Sales Page FAQ

### How technical is it?

Technical enough for senior AI PM credibility, but not a coding bootcamp. You will work with system maps, RAG, agents, evals, traces, cost, latency, and launch risk.

### What tools are used?

Named tools may include Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, n8n, Sheets/Airtable, Figma/FigJam, Lovable/v0/Bolt, and optional eval/tracing tools. The course teaches durable principles so the learning survives tool changes.

### What is the weekly workload?

2 hours live plus 2 to 4 hours of assignment work. Optional advanced labs may add 1 to 2 hours.

### What if I cannot share my company work?

Use an anonymized version, a public-domain example, or the shared Customer Success Copilot case.

### What is the final deliverable?

A Senior AI PM Portfolio Packet and interview narrative.
