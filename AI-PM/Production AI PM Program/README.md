# Production AI PM Program

## Lead GenAI and Agentic Products from Strategy to Launch

This folder contains the full program package for **Production AI PM**, a premium cohort program for senior PMs who want to lead GenAI and agentic products with credible product, technical, and launch judgment.

The program is designed for senior PMs targeting global or remote AI PM roles, and for product leaders who need to work effectively with AI/ML engineering teams.

---

## Program Promise

By the end, learners will be able to lead an AI product from strategy to production:

- choose AI opportunities worth pursuing;
- write an AI PRD with behavior, failure modes, and eval criteria;
- design a GenAI or agentic workflow with engineering;
- build or direct a low-code prototype;
- create a golden dataset and eval rubric;
- reason about cost, latency, safety, security, and launch readiness;
- present a senior AI PM portfolio and interview narrative.

---

## Format

- **Duration:** 8 weeks.
- **Live session:** 120 minutes per week.
- **Session style:** blended strategy + studio / critique.
- **Assignment load:** 2 to 4 hours per week.
- **Optional advanced lab:** 60 to 90 minutes for learners who want more technical depth.
- **Recommended cohort size:** 12 to 18 learners.

## Capstone Tracks

Learners choose one track:

1. **Enterprise Copilot** - best for B2B SaaS and internal AI products.
2. **Agentic Workflow Product** - best for operations, automation, and multi-step agents.
3. **Decision Support Product** - best for recommendations, personalization, trust, and evaluation.

The shared teaching case should use **Customer Success Copilot for B2B SaaS**, while learner assignments adapt the frameworks to their own company/product context when possible.
