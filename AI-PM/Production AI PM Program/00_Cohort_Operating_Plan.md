# Cohort Operating Plan

## Program Shape

**Production AI PM** runs as an 8-week live cohort program.

- 1 live session per week.
- 120 minutes per session.
- Blended strategy + studio / critique format.
- 2 to 4 hours of learner assignment work per week.
- Optional 60 to 90 minute advanced lab for technical learners.
- Recommended cohort size: 12 to 18 learners.

---

## Weekly Session Rhythm

Each 120-minute session uses this default flow:

| Segment | Time | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Opening and context | 0:00-0:10 | Set the session promise and connect to portfolio artifact |
| Strategy lens | 0:10-0:35 | Teach the core judgment model |
| Applied walkthrough | 0:35-0:55 | Show the framework on the shared case |
| Break | 0:55-1:05 | Real 10-minute break |
| Studio / critique | 1:05-1:45 | Learners apply the framework to capstones |
| Artifact close | 1:45-2:00 | Commit next assignment and review quality bar |

Adjust within 5 minutes as needed, but keep the strategy + studio blend in one session.

---

## Feedback Checkpoints

| Week | Feedback item | Review mode |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | AI Product Strategy Memo | Short written review or live critique |
| 4 | AI PRD + Workflow Prototype | Studio critique |
| 5 | Eval Dataset + Rubric | Rubric review |
| 7 | Production Readiness Review | Red-team review |
| 8 | Final Portfolio Packet | Final critique |

---

## Optional Premium Add-Ons

These can be sold as a higher-touch tier or offered selectively:

1. **1:1 capstone review**  
   A private review of the learner's portfolio packet, technical architecture, and launch readiness.

2. **Mock AI PM interview**  
   A senior AI PM interview simulation focused on product judgment, technical tradeoffs, evals, and stakeholder communication.

3. **Resume, LinkedIn, and portfolio positioning review**  
   Helps learners turn course artifacts into a credible external career signal.

---

## Pre-Cohort Setup

Before Week 1:

- Confirm learner profile and goals.
- Send tool setup instructions.
- Share the portfolio packet template.
- Ask learners to bring one real AI product idea from their work or domain.
- Prepare the shared B2B SaaS case.
- Prepare fallback capstone prompts for learners who cannot use work context.

---

## Post-Session Workflow

Within 24 hours after each session:

- Send the session workbook.
- Send the assignment reminder.
- Share the recording if applicable.
- Share any live critique notes.
- Add unresolved questions to a shared parking lot.
- Remind learners which portfolio artifact is due next.

---

## Facilitation Principles

- Assume learners already know PM fundamentals.
- Spend less time defining terms and more time applying judgment.
- Keep tools subordinate to product decisions.
- Treat evals, traces, cost, latency, risk, and launch gates as core PM craft.
- Push learners to make tradeoffs explicit.
