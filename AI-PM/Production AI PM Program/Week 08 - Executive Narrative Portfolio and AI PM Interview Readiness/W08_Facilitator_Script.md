# Facilitator Script - Executive Narrative, Portfolio, and AI PM Interview Readiness (Session 8 of 8)

**Subtitle:** Turn the portfolio into a credible senior AI PM signal.  
**Duration:** 120 minutes. **Format:** Live virtual. **Audience:** Senior PMs and product leaders.  
**Spine:** Portfolio Bar -> Executive Narrative -> Interview Story -> Final Critique.  
**Hands-on share:** ~55% (about 66 minutes).  
**Session artifact:** Final Senior AI PM Portfolio Packet and AI PM Interview Narrative.  
**Tools assumed available:** Final portfolio packet, learner artifacts, optional public/private portfolio page.

---

## Pre-Class Checklist

- [ ] Ask learners to submit portfolio packet before class if possible.
- [ ] Select 2-3 artifacts for anonymized live critique.
- [ ] Prepare executive narrative example.
- [ ] Prepare AI PM interview prompt set.

---

# The 120-Minute Blended Run Sheet

## Block 0 - Opening (0:00-0:10)

**Say:** "The portfolio is not a folder of classwork. It is evidence that you can lead an AI product through ambiguity."

Ask chat: "What do you most want this portfolio to prove?"

## Block 1 - Strategy Lens: The Senior AI PM Story (0:10-0:30)

Teach the narrative spine:

1. Business problem.
2. Why AI / why now.
3. Product strategy.
4. System design.
5. Evals and quality bar.
6. Production risk.
7. Leadership tradeoffs.

## Block 2 - Applied Walkthrough: Board Memo And Interview Story (0:30-0:55)

Show the same capstone framed two ways:

- Executive version: concise, outcome and risk focused.
- Interview version: judgment, tradeoffs, engineering collaboration, evals.

Contrast weak vs strong storytelling:

- Weak: "I built an AI agent."
- Strong: "I identified a workflow where AI could reduce account review time, designed the approval-gated system, defined evals, and set launch gates for cost, risk, and quality."

## Hard Break (0:55-1:05)

Real break.

## Block 3 - Studio: Portfolio Critique (1:05-1:38)

Learners review each other's packet:

- What proves senior judgment?
- What sounds like tool usage?
- What artifact is strongest?
- What artifact needs evidence?
- What should be public, private, or anonymized?

Review 2 examples live.

## Block 4 - Interview Narrative Rehearsal (1:38-1:55)

Learners practice a 2-minute version:

1. Problem.
2. Strategy.
3. System.
4. Evals.
5. Launch risk.
6. Leadership.

Peer gives one improvement.

## Block 5 - Close (1:55-2:00)

Final instruction:

- Complete final portfolio packet.
- Decide public/private/anonymized version.
- Use optional premium add-ons if purchased: 1:1 review, mock interview, resume/LinkedIn/portfolio review.

---

## Contingencies

**If portfolios are incomplete:** focus on the story and next 7-day polish plan.  
**If learners overemphasize tools:** ask what judgment the tool use proves.  
**If learners are nervous about public artifacts:** help them create anonymized versions.
