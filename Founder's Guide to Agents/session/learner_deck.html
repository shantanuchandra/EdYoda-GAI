Frame·Agent vs. Prompt
Founder's Guide to Agents
The real test

A prompt with a costume on is not an agent.

Question

Which of the three tests does your current AI workflow fail?

1

Acts on live data

Not just what you typed — something it pulls itself: your repo, a live web search, your inbox.

2

Takes real action in another system

Sends the email, writes the file, updates the record. Doesn't just hand you text.

3

Doesn't wait to be re-prompted

Runs on a trigger — a schedule, a webhook, an event.
